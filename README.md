# prompt-design
You are an expert F&B Operations Consultant and Student Micro-Venture Strategist. 

### CONTEXT & CHALLENGE
We have a strict working capital budget of ₹10,000 to revamp and operate a college canteen for exactly 1 week (5 working days, Monday–Friday). The goal is to maximize student satisfaction, eliminate food waste, achieve high margin reinvestment, and ensure seamless queue management during short 15–30 minute lecture breaks.

### OBJECTIVE
Develop a complete 1-week tactical operational blueprint covering menu engineering, cost breakdown, unit economics, demand forecasting, and inventory flow.

---

### INSTRUCTIONS & REASONING FRAMEWORK

Please reason through the following 5 phases step-by-step:

#### 1. Menu Engineering (High Margin + Fast Prep)
- Select 4–5 items based on the "Star/Plowhorse" matrix:
  - 1 Grab-and-Go breakfast item (e.g., Poha/Idli or Masala Bun).
  - 2 High-margin crowd-pullers (e.g., Peri-Peri Maggi/Loaded Fries, Cold Coffee/Kulhad Chai).
  - 1 Healthy/Diet-conscious option (e.g., Sprouts Chaat or Protein Smoothie).
  - 1 Daily "Flash Special" to induce FOMO.
- Criteria: Must take <90 seconds to assemble or be pre-batch cooked to eliminate wait times.

#### 2. Detailed Budget Allocation & Unit Economics (₹10,000 Cap)
Break down the ₹10,000 budget into:
- Raw Material & Ingredients: Max 65% (₹6,500)
- Packaging & Hygiene (Eco-friendly paper boxes/cups): 15% (₹1,500)
- Marketing & Pop-up Setup (Signage/Chalkboards): 10% (₹1,000)
- Contingency / Spoilage Buffer: 10% (₹1,000)
*Provide a table showing: Item Name | Raw Cost (COGS) | Selling Price (₹) | Target Gross Margin (%) | Target Daily Quantity.*

#### 3. Surge & Demand Management (Zero Bottlenecks)
Propose an innovative, low-cost mechanism to prevent chaotic canteen rushes between classes:
- Token/Slot or Pre-order System (e.g., Google Form / WhatsApp ordering before 11:00 AM).
- Express Grab-and-Go Lane for UPI payments.
- Dynamic "Happy Hour" pricing for slow slots (10:00–11:00 AM) to balance peak noon traffic.

#### 4. Waste & Perishability Mitigation (Critical Thinking)
- How do we handle leftover perishable stock each day? (e.g., Evening 4:00 PM clearance discounts, dry-ingredient stocking strategy).
- Inventory velocity: Buy ingredients on a rolling 2-day cycle rather than all on Day 1 to preserve cash flow.

---

### REQUIRED OUTPUT FORMAT
Deliver the solution in clear, executive-grade Markdown:
1. **Executive Strategy & Philosophy** (Target student persona & brand positioning)
2. **Menu & Financial Unit Economics Table** (COGS, Selling Price, Margin %, Daily Projected Revenue)
3. **Daily Demand & Shift Schedule** (Morning Rush, Peak Lunch, Afternoon Slump)
4. **Demand-Control Mechanism** (Step-by-step queue & ordering system)
5. **Projected 1-Week P&L Statement** (Total Revenue, Net Profit, ROI on the ₹10,000 seed capital)
